Building by building

The town starts in ruins, the blast's crystal still burning through it. Repair a building and it opens the next. Raise the mage's tower and the whole town can grow.

The mage's tower at levels 1 to 5, drawn by the game.

Raise a team of heroes

Each hero was changed by the blast in their own way, and each wants something from what is left.

Three heroes in the first test; more are planned for later releases. Portraits from each hero's page in the game.

Take in the changed

The blast changed whoever it reached. Win the forest's fights to rescue a group's king or queen, and their folk come home with you, to be housed and put to work. They become the troops your heroes lead.

Pictures from each group's page in the game.

See it in motion

Fights play out on their own. Pick your heroes and send them down the road; each stage won takes the squad further.

Closing the game is always safe

Your town banks what it earns for up to ten hours, and nothing is taken from it while you're gone.
