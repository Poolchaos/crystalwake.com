Crystalwake

A town to rebuild after the blast

A magical blast levelled your town. Rebuild it building by building around the mage's tower, take in the creatures and survivors the blast left behind, and raise a team of heroes to push back into the wasteland.

Crystalwake is in development for Android phones and tablets, on Google Play.
